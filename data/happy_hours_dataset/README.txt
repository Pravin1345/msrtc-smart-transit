HAPPY HOURS DATASET (SYNTHETIC, for testing the pipeline)
Period: trips from 2024-01-01 to 2025-12-31. 2,500 trips, 8 routes, 45 seats, base fare Rs 450 for every trip.
All rows are generated by a script. Nothing here is real MSRTC data.

FILES
locations_master      8 stops (Pune, Kolhapur, Satara, Karad, Sangli, Solapur, Nashik, Mumbai)
routes                8 directed routes with distance_km
calendar_features     one row per day: weekend, holiday, festival, college peak, season
trips_master          one row per trip: departure_time, booking_open_time, pricing_start_time (-48h), booking_close_time (-1h)
users                 800 users, category general / women / senior / medical
bookings_history      one row per booking (1 seat each). online or manual, confirmed or cancelled, points used, amount paid
priority_safety       reserved seats per trip (women 3, senior 2, medical 1) and priority seats already booked
loyalty_accounts      final points balance per user
loyalty_transactions  every earn and redeem. Earn = floor(amount paid / 10). Redeem: 1 point = Rs 0.5, fare never below Rs 300
training_table        THE FILE THE MODEL TRAINS ON. One row per trip per snapshot (48h to 1h before departure)
fare_response         SYNTHETIC price reaction per candidate fare (assumed, replace after a real pilot)

HOW THE DATA WAS MADE (so you can trust or doubt each part)
- History is "before Happy Hours": every confirmed online booking paid Rs 450 (minus points). So demand at lower fares is unknown.
- current_bookings in training_table = confirmed ONLINE bookings made earlier than that snapshot (counted from bookings_history).
- Targets: online_bookings_at_close, offline_boardings (manual after online closed), final_occupancy_percent. Cancelled bookings are not counted.
- historical_avg_occupancy and loyalty_user_ratio use only EARLIER trips on the same route (no future leakage).
- Manual passengers have no user_id and earn no points. About 5% of bookings are cancelled; they are not in loyalty_transactions.
- fare_response uses an assumed elasticity (online +6.0 x discount, manual -2.5 x discount). It is NOT real behaviour.
